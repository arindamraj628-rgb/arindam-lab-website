# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-wrench" %} Research Themes and Projects

Interested in developing rapid multiscale characterization and synthesis techniques with the ultimate aim to design damage-tolerant structural materials. I study transport phenomena with high resolution in alloy microstructures, develop synthesis and fabrication methods for multiscale material designs, to enable the emergent mechanical properties for materials which can mitigate damage, accommodate defects, and autonomously heal defects.
